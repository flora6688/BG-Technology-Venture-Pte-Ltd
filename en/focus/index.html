OUR DIRECTION

Our planned business areas

Proposed areas of development, shaped around the ways businesses collect, pay and connect.

i
Our current stage

BG Technology Venture Pte Ltd is currently in the business planning and preparation stage and has not obtained a payment services licence from the Monetary Authority of Singapore. This website describes proposed areas of development. The payment services described are not available, and no account applications, transactions or funds are accepted for these services. Any future launch of regulated payment services is subject to obtaining the applicable licences and meeting relevant requirements.

01
Planned

Enterprise cross-border payments

We plan to explore cross-border payment solutions for businesses, with a focus on international trade, supplier payments and cross-border commercial activity.

Areas of interest

  • International trade
  • Supplier payments
  • Cross-border commerce

What we plan to explore

Our planning will consider the information businesses need to understand payment progress, manage counterparties and reconcile commercial payments.

02
Planned

Merchant payment solutions

We plan to explore payment acceptance and settlement solutions for online merchants and digital platforms, focusing on integration, transaction visibility and reconciliation.

Areas of interest

  • Online merchants
  • Digital platforms
  • Payment reconciliation

What we plan to explore

We aim to evaluate merchant needs across payment integration, transaction information and settlement workflows. Available payment methods and markets have not been determined.

03
Planned

Payment technology & integration

We plan to explore API connectivity, payment workflows and reconciliation tools to support future business payment use cases.

Areas of interest

  • API connectivity
  • Payment workflows
  • Reconciliation tools

What we plan to explore

Our technology planning will focus on clear information flows and integration requirements. No live API, production platform or integration service is currently offered through this website.
